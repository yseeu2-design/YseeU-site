My name is Yvonne Cloete.

Yvonne Cloete

Growing up in South Africa, Yvonne saw nearly everything that happened to her as a punishment — a sign she wasn't good enough. Years of study, teaching, and her own inner work led her somewhere different: the idea that Earth runs on a curriculum of duality, and that we begin in darkness only so we can find our way, deliberately, toward the light.

In 1997 she moved her three daughters — then 14, 12, and 10 — from South Africa to the United States. She completed her coursework toward a Ph.D. in Industrial-Organizational Psychology, then left a career managing a reconstructive plastic surgeon's office to move to Spain and write her book, See The Gift Not The Curse. She's flown as a flight attendant ever since — today with Southwest Airlines — and is currently revamping the YseeU brand with new teaching, on the road and in the air.

Her purpose: to help people learn to enhance and trust the next five senses of the right brain — the ones humanity has left largely unexplored.
